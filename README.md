A classic 2-player Tic-Tac-Toe game built in **C++** for the terminal/console. Play locally against a friend, featuring clean board rendering, input validation, and win/draw detection!

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎮 **2-Player Mode**: Play head-to-head on a single computer.
- 🛡️ **Input Validation**: Prevents invalid moves or overwriting occupied positions.
- 🏆 **Smart Win Detection**: Automatically checks rows, columns, and diagonals after every turn.
- 🤝 **Draw Detection**: Recognizes full board states when no moves remain.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1. The game is played on a standard **3x3 grid**.
2. **Player 1** plays as `X` (or the symbol he desires) and **Player 2** plays as `O`(or the symbol he desires).
3. Players take turns selecting a position (1–9) on the board.
4. First player to align 3 of their symbols horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins!

 1 | 2 | 3 
---|---|---
 4 | 5 | 6 
---|---|---
 7 | 8 | 9
